My Journey Through CACSA OUI

Omoniyi Victoria shares the story of how God transformed her faith, academics, leadership, and daily life.

Faith & DevotionBy Omoniyi VictoriaPublished October 11, 20267 min read

MY TESTIMONY: MY JOURNEY THROUGH CACSA OUI ❤️🥹

When I first stepped into CACSA OUI, I honestly had no idea what God had in store for me. My plan was simple: come to school, graduate and leave. I had no big expectations, but God had a beautiful plan for me.

The first time I walked into CACSA, I encountered a house full of love. I was welcomed with open arms, and for the first time, I felt like I wasn’t far away from home. The joy in the house, the love among the brethren and especially the way they prayed in tongues captured my heart. Little did I know that this family would become a significant part of my testimony.

Looking back today, I can only say that God has been faithful!

Transformed by the family

I came into CACSA as someone who was shy and struggled with prayer, but God used this family to transform me. He helped me become more confident, express myself freely and grow spiritually. My relationship with Him deepened, my prayer life improved and I became a better version of myself.

God also showed Himself mighty in my academics. After joining CACSA, I recorded my first 5.0 out of 5.0. Even while serving as the Sisters’ Coordinator, my CGPA never dropped.

Grace for every responsibility

When I became President, I honestly thought my academic performance would suffer because of the numerous responsibilities I had to handle.

Without a Vice President, I had so much on my plate. I was juggling responsibilities in CACSA, JCCF, my department, the Osun State Department and many more. There were moments when everything felt overwhelming, and I wondered how I would cope.

But God came through for me!

Despite all the responsibilities, I recorded a 5.0 in both semesters of my final year. Out of the six semesters I completed in my department, I had four 5.0 CGPAs.

Sometimes, when I look back at everything I handled and what God helped me achieve, I am overwhelmed with gratitude. I know it was not by my strength alone. God sustained me!

God's provision

And then there is my testimony of God’s provision.

There were times I sacrificed my feeding money to support God’s work. There were moments when I had just ₦100 left in my account. Yet, somehow, someone would call and send me money 🥹

These may sound like little things to some people, but to me, they were reminders that God saw me. He saw my sacrifices, He knew my needs and He provided for me in different ways.

Sometimes, I sit down and wonder what my story would have been if I had never walked through those doors. All I know is that I am grateful God led me here.

What I have learnt

Today, I look at my journey and see more than academic results or leadership positions. I see the faithfulness of God. I see a girl who came into university with no idea of what the future held and a God who used the journey to transform her.

To someone who is like me, whether you’re a fresher, an executive or a worker, there is something I have learnt through this journey: When you take God’s matter seriously, trust Him and serve Him wholeheartedly, He will also take your matter seriously.

Believe me, I have seen His faithfulness in my spiritual life, academics and finances, and I am forever grateful.

If God can do it for me, He can do it for you too!

THANK YOU, JESUS! ❤️🥹

— Omoniyi Victoria
