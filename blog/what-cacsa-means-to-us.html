What CACSA Means to Us

CACSA means Christ Apostolic Church Students Association. At Oduduwa University, that name describes a community where students can encounter Christ, grow in faith, and learn to serve others with love and integrity.

We often describe ourselves as a family of grace: people who are being helped by God and who make room for one another. That identity shapes how we worship, how we welcome students, and how we walk through university life together.

What we are committed to

  • Making Christ known through campus evangelism, outreaches, and everyday witness.
  • Raising disciples who are grounded in the Word of God, fervent in prayer, and committed to holy Christian living.
  • Providing a spiritual family of grace where students feel loved, supported, and nurtured to fulfil Godâ€™s purpose.
  • Manifesting the kingdom of God in power, character, service, and culture on campus.
  • Fostering academic excellence and character as part of a whole and faithful student life.
  • Ensuring generational continuity by raising and transitioning faithful stewards to carry the vision forward.

Why it matters

University is more than a season of classes and examinations. It is also a season of formation. CACSA OUI exists to help students become people who know Christ, love people, serve faithfully, and carry godly influence into every part of life.

CACSA is not just a family we attend. It is a family we help build, one generation at a time.

That is what CACSA means to us: Christ at the centre, people held together by grace, and a generation learning to follow well.
