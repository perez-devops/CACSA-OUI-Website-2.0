Church History

The story of CACSA OUI began in 2013, when the CACSA OAU Mission Subgroup started reaching students of Oduduwa University through evangelism. In November 2014, CACSA OUI was officially planted with a small team of OUI and OAU students, with Bro. Sanya serving as the first head of the team. The leadership later passed through Bro. Emmanuel Olowokere, Bro. Jeremiah and Bro. Pelumi Emmanuel as the work continued to grow.

In 2016, CACSA OUI became an independent student fellowship within Oduduwa University, with Bro. Adeyemi John Ademola becoming the first OUI student President. That same year, the fellowship celebrated its first graduating student. In 2017, CACSA OUI held its first Freshers' Welcome Service, welcoming a new generation of students into the fellowship.

In April 2018, the fellowship held its first KLC and Anniversary and welcomed the National Director, Pastor Dare Caleb, and the then State Coordinator, Pastor Ruben. In June 2018, the pioneer President handed over to Bro. Zion Oyedele Anuoluwapo, beginning a continued pattern of leadership transition and growth.

Since then, CACSA OUI has been led by different generations of students: Bro. Zion Oyedele Anuoluwapo (2018–2020), Sis. Grace Adewunmi (2020–2021), Sis. Olaniyan Lois (2021–2024), Sis. Sinmileoluwa (2024–2025), Sis. Victoria (2025–2026), and Bro. God'sPower Emmanuel (2026–Present).

Through every season, the vision has remained the same: to make Christ known, raise disciples, and provide a family where students can grow in their walk with God and fulfil His purpose for their lives. From a small mission outreach to an established student fellowship, CACSA OUI continues to be a testimony of God's faithfulness, built from one generation to another.

Vision and Mission Statements

  1. 1.

    To make Christ known across Oduduwa University through regular campus evangelism, outreaches, and kingdom influence.

  2. 2.

    To raise disciples who are deeply grounded in the undiluted Word of God, fervent prayer, and holy Christian living.

  3. 3.

    To provide a spiritual family of grace where every student feels loved, supported, and nurtured to fulfill God’s purpose for their lives.

  4. 4.

    To manifest the kingdom of God in power and culture in a depraved generation, shining as lights on the university campus.

  5. 5.

    To uphold the doctrines and apostolic heritage of Christ Apostolic Church, walking in holiness, faith, and spiritual power.

  6. 6.

    To foster academic excellence and character integrity, preparing students to be godly leaders and nation builders.

  7. 7.

    To ensure generational continuity, raising and transitioning faithful stewards to carry the vision from one generation to another.

We are a Family of Grace, a people of Love, the Word of God and Prayer. Birthed from the doctrines and values of CAC to reveal Christ and manifest God's kingdom in power and culture on campus.
