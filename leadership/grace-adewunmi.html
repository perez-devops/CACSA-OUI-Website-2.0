THE TENURE

Tenure of Sis. Grace Adewunmi

Guiding the fellowship through critical transition, anchoring resilient altars of prayer, and maintaining vibrant fellowship unity.

The 2020 – 2021 session was marked by unusual global and academic disruptions, yet under the pastoral grace of Sis. Grace Adewunmi, the CACSA OUI altar burned brighter than ever.

Her team rallied students through relentless prayer chains, pastoral follow-up, and unyielding mutual care, ensuring that not a single member was lost to the vicissitudes of the season.

“In every transition, God's grace proved sufficient to sustain His altar and keep His children anchored in love.”
Sis. Grace Adewunmi PRESIDENT, CACSA OUI • 2020 – 2021
