THE TENURE

Tenure of Sis. Olaniyan Lois

A milestone three-year tenure of persistent campus evangelism, expanding fellowship roots, and establishing enduring discipleship structures.

Spanning three landmark academic sessions from 2021 to 2024, the administration of Sis. Olaniyan Lois represented an era of resilience, stabilization, and generational expansion for CACSA OUI.

Through institutional stability and steadfast dedication, her council fortified fellowship departments, introduced enduring campus outreach initiatives, and nurtured multiple classes of freshmen into consecrated Christian workers.

“Faithful stewardship that anchored the identity of CACSA on Oduduwa University campus across changing seasons.”
Sis. Olaniyan Lois PRESIDENTIAL ADMINISTRATION • 2021 – 2024
